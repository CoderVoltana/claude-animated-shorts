# SimTheory #05 · CAN A PENDULUM FLIP FOREVER?

## Title (44 characters)
Can a Pendulum Flip Forever? | SimTheory #05

## Description
Nine identical pendulums get nine different pushes: some stay trapped, some flip over the top forever, and one gets exactly enough energy to creep towards the top for all time without ever arriving.
Real physics: nonlinear pendulum simulation; the threshold case uses the exact analytic solution, shown in slow-motion replay.
